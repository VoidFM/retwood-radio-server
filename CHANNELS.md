# Clearfield County channel profile

48 simulator channels, organized into three 16-channel zones:

- **Clearfield Co:** Dispatch, Fire Ground 1–12 and Operations 13–15. These keep the original file’s Dispatch and Fire Ground 1–3 labels.
- **Clearfield Co EMS:** EMS Dispatch, Med 5 DuBois, Med 6 Clearfield and supporting EMS operations channels.
- **Clearfield Co TAC:** County Ops, command, incident operations, rescue, mutual aid, training and direct channels.

Clearfield County’s published fire list names Fire Dispatch, Fire Ground 2–12, Operations 13–15 and County Ops. Fire Ground 1 is retained as an additional simulator channel. Zone grouping, extra EMS/TAC aliases and their positions are custom simulator configuration, not a verified county radio codeplug.

The county’s published EMS procedure names Med 9 for EMS dispatch, Med 5 for DuBois hospital notification and Med 6 for Clearfield hospital notification/dispatch backup. The Med 5/6 labels here are based on that procedure, dated October 2005; they do not assert current hospital names or assignments.

Sources:

- Clearfield County Fire Radio Dispatch System: https://clearfieldcountypa.gov/293/Fire-Radio-Dispatch-System
- County EMS procedures index: https://clearfieldcountypa.gov/302/EMS
- Published EMS frequency procedure: https://www.clearfield911.com/content/emssogs/File/6%2002%20EMS%20Radio%20Frequencies.pdf

This file changes simulator aliases only. No frequencies, tones, access credentials or real radio programming are configured by it.
